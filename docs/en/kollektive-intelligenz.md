# CI-0.1 · Collective intelligence as a founding idea of WIRKLICH

**Architecture hypothesis · 7 November 2026 · unvalidated English draft**

> **WIRKLICH should not think on humanity's behalf. It should help people think better together – question by question, correction by correction.**

The six atlas fields – reality, life, consciousness, the human, society and action – organize **questions and domains, not brain areas or cognitive modules**. The parallel with language models is *functional*: questions as inputs, versioned claims as external memory, proposed connections, objections, and revision through feedback. Today's GitHub prototype does **not** have shared neural weights, autonomous collective learning or consciousness.

**Research hypothesis:** On bounded tasks, coordination between diverse people, transparent knowledge relationships and limited AI assistance may improve real error detection, cross-domain transfer and reasoned revision compared with practicable alternatives. This is unproven.

## Six functions and feedback

1. **Receive:** People contribute questions, perspectives and neglected voices. AI may propose drafts but cannot simulate human support.
2. **Remember:** Preserve provenance, stable IDs, claims, sources, objections and revision history.
3. **Connect:** The atlas, question atlas and navigator propose links whose applicability must be checked anew.
4. **Examine:** META-01 distinguishes empirical, formal, normative, interpretive and metaphorical claims, seeking the strongest objection.
5. **Account:** Responsible humans decide what is published; preserve appeal and reasoned minority dissent.
6. **Feed back:** Measure outcomes, errors, harm and cost; reopen questions and rules when warranted.

It is a revisable **loop**, not a hierarchy of human value or a mechanism for compulsory consensus.

## What exists today

The atlas and topic tree are bilingual static maps; the question atlas has six manually curated examples and no full live sync; the claims register tracks early drafts; the navigator offers four human-authored pattern cards and local text matching; bounded AI swarms produce research drafts without automatic editorial authority. **No independent evidence of collective learning, autonomous knowledge graph or representative governance exists yet.**

## Risks and tests

A crowd can amplify conformity, privilege dominant voices, manufacture false cross-domain analogies and overload attention. A thousand correlated agents do not form a thousand independent reviewers. A separate [moral direction index in open PR #73](https://github.com/AlexanderR1991/Wirklich/pull/73) is an unvalidated proposal, **not** an automatic output or human ranking.

We must compare real error detection and false alarms against ordinary source checking, test cross-domain matches, preserve consequential minority objections and ask whether later decisions improve rather than merely becoming longer and costlier. Criteria must be specified beforehand. Null or harmful results should narrow or overturn the hypothesis.

**The collective brings perspectives. The atlas records relations. The method asks for reasons. AI may assist and challenge. People remain accountable. Reality can correct us all.**
